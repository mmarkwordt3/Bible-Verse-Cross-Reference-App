## Brenton English Septuagint review assistance

The classic Brenton English Septuagint, source ID `eng-Brenton`, is distributed by eBible.org as public-domain USFM. Source/version, retrieval state, archive URL, and SHA-256 pin are recorded in `data/sources/brenton-source.json`. It is used only as review-assistance display text and is not generated, algorithmically translated, or represented as matching every CenterBLC recension.
